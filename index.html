MEDIAOS is the private production tool behind Mind Wired, an educational YouTube channel about psychology and brain science. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account that signs in to it is the channel owner's.

What it does

  • Drafts short and long educational videos. Every factual claim is checked against web sources, and long videos list their sources in the description.
  • Narrates them, adds licensed stock footage (Pixabay, Pexels) and captions, and renders the video.
  • Holds each video for the channel owner to review and approve before it is published. Once the owner has approved five in a row, an auto-approve rule they can switch off publishes videos that passed every check, or whose only flag is a fact check that confirmed most of its claims and contradicted none; any other flagged video still waits for them.
  • Uploads approved videos to the owner's own channel with the YouTube Data API, sets their thumbnails, posts a comment on each, and adds long videos to playlists on the channel.
  • Replies, as the channel, to viewers' public comments on its recent videos, with short answers drafted from the video's own script.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics so the owner can see what works, and uses that to plan future videos and posting times.

MEDIAOS uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the privacy policy for what data it uses and how to have it deleted, and the Google Privacy Policy.
